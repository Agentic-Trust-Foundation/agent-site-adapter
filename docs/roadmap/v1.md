## Definition of Done for V1

V1 should not be declared complete until:
- security model is documented
- identity binding is testable
- capability declarations are unambiguous
- trust evidence is verifiable
- revocation/versioning are defined
- ATF integration is explicit
- payment handoff does not expand authority
- conformance tests exist
- implementation and protocol boundaries are documented

## Phase 6 — Authorization, Consent, and Delegation

**Status:** Complete — 2026-09-21

Completed architecture contracts:

- authorization as contextual evaluation rather than an agent-wide boolean;
- capability versus authority separation;
- capability/action/resource/parameter matching;
- bounded delegation and authority attenuation;
- audience/resource/service binding;
- security-sensitive parameter binding;
- consent as policy evidence rather than authority creation;
- semantic outcomes: ALLOW, DENY, REQUIRE_HUMAN;
- human approval boundary;
- authority lifecycle and fail-closed behavior;
- Agent-Pay handoff without authority expansion;
- authority evidence binding and substitution resistance;
- semantic audit/correlation requirements.

The exact credential, delegation-chain, consent, policy, and wire serialization remain profile-specific until a later normative interoperability profile freezes them.

Phase 9 must convert these semantics into executable conformance cases.
