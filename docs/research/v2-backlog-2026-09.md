# Site Adapter V2 Research Backlog

**Status:** Research backlog only — non-normative

## 1. Federation

Study federation and trust-domain discovery without creating a mandatory global registry.

## 2. Advanced delegation

Study richer multi-hop delegation, attenuation languages, constrained sub-delegation and revocation propagation.

## 3. Verifiable claims

Study selective-disclosure credentials and stronger provenance/assurance evidence. No credential format is adopted by V2 backlog work yet.

## 4. Cross-domain trust

Study explicit trust-domain policy, issuer discovery, trust anchors and policy composition.

## 5. Reputation and assurance

Study portable assurance signals and reputation while keeping reputation separate from authorization.

## 6. Agent-to-agent authorization

Study long-running tasks, asynchronous delegation, task-scoped authority and cross-agent resource binding.

## 7. Ecosystem governance

Study extension registries, version governance, interoperability certification and change control.

## V2 rule

Nothing in this backlog changes V1 behavior. Any adopted mechanism requires a new normative specification, security review and conformance update.
